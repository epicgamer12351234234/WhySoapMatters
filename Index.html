The perfect wash.

Four simple steps. No complicated science degree required.

01 — WET

Get your hands wet

Use clean, running water.

02 — SOAP

Add soap

Cover your hands with soap.

03 — SCRUB

Scrub well

Rub palms, backs, fingers and under your nails for about 20 seconds.

04 — RINSE

Rinse & dry

Rinse thoroughly and dry your hands.
